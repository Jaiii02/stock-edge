# StockEdge Investment Framework

## Objective

Build a long-term investment intelligence system that identifies:

- Good businesses
- Attractive valuations
- Strong momentum
- Favorable market conditions

---

# Fundamental Factors

## Valuation

### PE Ratio

Status: Implemented

Meaning:
Price paid per rupee of earnings.

Why included:
Cheaper companies often provide better risk/reward.

Direction:
Lower = Better

---

### EV/EBITDA

Status: Planned

Meaning:
Enterprise value relative to operating profits.

Why included:
Less affected by capital structure.

Direction:
Lower = Better

---

### FCF Yield

Status: Planned

Meaning:
Free cash flow relative to company value.

Why included:
Measures actual cash generation.

Direction:
Higher = Better

---

# Quality

### ROE

Status: Implemented

Meaning:
Return generated on shareholder capital.

Why included:
Measures business quality.

Direction:
Higher = Better

---

### ROCE

Status: Planned

Meaning:
Efficiency of capital employed.

Why included:
Preferred metric for capital-intensive businesses.

Direction:
Higher = Better

---

# Growth

### Revenue Growth

Status: Implemented

Meaning:
Growth in company sales.

Why included:
Shows expansion potential.

Direction:
Higher = Better

---

### Earnings Growth

Status: Planned

Meaning:
Growth in profits.

Why included:
Revenue growth alone is insufficient.

Direction:
Higher = Better

---

# Risk

### Debt-to-Equity

Status: Implemented

Meaning:
Leverage used by company.

Why included:
High debt increases risk.

Direction:
Lower = Better

---

### Interest Coverage

Status: Planned

Meaning:
Ability to service debt.

Why included:
Measures financial safety.

Direction:
Higher = Better
